Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026 · Fort Worth Dentist · fortworthdentist.com

What we collect

When you use this website, we may collect:

  • Information you submit — your name, phone number, email address, and anything you write in our appointment request form or type to our chat assistant.
  • Usage and advertising data — cookies and similar identifiers collected automatically through Google Tag Manager and Google Ads tags, such as pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate location, and ad-click identifiers (for example, a Google Click ID) used to measure whether our advertising works.

Please do not submit detailed medical or health information through this website or the chat assistant. This site is for scheduling and general questions; clinical matters are discussed privately at the practice. This website is not a medical record system.

How we use it

We use the information you submit to respond to you, schedule appointments, and communicate about your care. We use usage and advertising data to understand how the site is used and to measure and improve our advertising. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to running the practice.

Who processes it for us

Like most modern websites, we rely on service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Formspree delivers form and chat submissions to our practice email.
  • Google (Tag Manager, Ads, Workspace email) provides advertising measurement and our email service.
  • Anthropic processes chat assistant conversations to generate responses.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub host and serve this website securely.

Each processes data only to provide its service to us, under its own security and privacy commitments.

Cookies and choices

Advertising and measurement cookies are set by Google tags on this site. You can limit ad personalization at Google Ads Settings, clear or block cookies in your browser settings, and use private browsing to avoid persistent cookies.

Your rights

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (subject to records we're required to keep). Texas residents have these rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act. To make a request, email admin@fortworthdentist.com or call 817-926-1300.

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information online from children under 13; appointment requests for minors should be made by a parent or guardian.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update it here with a new effective date. Questions: admin@fortworthdentist.com · 817-926-1300 · Fort Worth Dentist, 1612 Pennsylvania Ave (through Aug 31, 2026), then 416 S Henderson St, Fort Worth, TX 76104.
