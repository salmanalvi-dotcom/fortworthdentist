Chipped a tooth? Here are your repair options

5 min read · Fort Worth Dentist

Most chips are fixed in one visit. The right repair depends on how much tooth is missing, where it is, and whether the nerve is involved — and the difference between options is bigger than most people expect.

Small chip on an edge: bonding

Tooth-colored composite resin is shaped directly onto the tooth and cured with light, usually in under an hour and often without anesthetic. It's the least invasive and least expensive option, and for a minor front-tooth chip it's frequently indistinguishable from the original. Composite does stain and wear faster than porcelain over the years, so it's a maintenance-friendly choice rather than a permanent one.

Larger break: a crown

When a significant piece has broken away, or the tooth is cracked, bonding won't hold — the repair needs to encircle and reinforce the tooth. A crown restores full strength and shape. Expect two visits: an iTero digital scan and preparation first, then fitting the finished crown. More on dental crowns.

Front tooth where appearance matters most: a veneer

Porcelain veneers give the most natural and longest-lasting result on front teeth, particularly when the chip sits alongside other cosmetic concerns like shape or shade. For minimal prep porcelain veneer work, Dr. Khan's boutique studio, The Veneer Suite, focuses exclusively on this.

If the break reached the nerve: root canal, then crown

Sharp pain, lingering sensitivity to hot or cold, or visible pink or red at the break usually means the pulp is exposed. The tooth is treated first to remove the damaged nerve tissue, then protected with a crown. Untreated, this reliably becomes an abscess — this is the scenario where waiting costs the most.

How the decision actually gets made

We photograph the tooth, check whether the nerve is responding normally, and look at how your bite lands on it — a chip on a tooth taking heavy bite force needs a sturdier repair than the same chip on a tooth that doesn't. You see the photo, you hear the options with clear pricing, and you decide.

Common questions

How is a chipped tooth fixed?+

Small chips are repaired with tooth-colored bonding in a single visit. Larger breaks usually need a crown. A chipped front tooth may be best restored with a porcelain veneer, and if the break reached the nerve, root canal treatment comes first.

Is a chipped tooth an emergency?+

A small chip without pain can usually wait a few days, though sharp edges can cut your tongue and exposed inner tooth decays quickly. A large break, pain on biting, or bleeding should be seen the same day.

Why do my teeth keep chipping?+

Repeated chipping usually points to an underlying cause — nighttime grinding, a bite problem, acid erosion, or old fillings that have weakened the surrounding tooth. Finding the cause prevents it recurring.
