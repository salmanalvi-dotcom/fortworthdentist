Modern dentistry

Technology is our ally.

A fully digital practice isn't about gadgets — it's about precision you can see, earlier detection, and visits that feel calmer because nothing is guesswork.

Digital X-rays & 3D imaging

Low-radiation digital imaging shows us what film never could — and shows you too, on screen, so findings are something we look at together rather than something you take on faith.

Digital scanning — goodbye, goopy impressions

A digital scanner captures a precise 3D model of your teeth in minutes, no trays of impression material. It's how Invisalign cases are planned, how crowns are designed to fit right the first time, and how implants are mapped before anything is placed.

Intraoral cameras

Small HD cameras capture detailed images inside your mouth, so you can see exactly what we see — a cracked filling or early decay explained with a photo, not just a pointing finger.

Virtual smile simulation

For cosmetic work, digital smile simulation lets you preview your projected result before committing to anything — the same see-it-first philosophy behind Dr. Khan's veneer work at The Veneer Suite.
