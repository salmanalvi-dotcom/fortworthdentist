Dental implants

A dental implant replaces the whole tooth — root and crown — so it looks, feels, and works like your own. It's the closest modern dentistry comes to growing a tooth back.

How implants work

A small titanium post takes the place of the missing root and integrates with your jawbone over a few months. A custom crown is then attached, matched to your surrounding teeth in shape and shade. Unlike a bridge, an implant doesn't rely on grinding down healthy neighboring teeth, and unlike a denture, it doesn't move.

Planned digitally, placed precisely

Dr. Khan is Implant Continuum trained with more than 2,000 hours of continuing education across implantology and restorative dentistry. Every case begins with digital imaging and planning, so placement is mapped before anything happens in your mouth — whether it's one tooth, several, or a full-arch solution.

Are you a candidate?

Most adults with a missing or failing tooth are. Candidacy comes down to gum health and available bone, which we assess with imaging at your consultation. If an implant isn't your best option, you'll hear that honestly, along with the alternatives — that consultation is a diagnosis, not a sales pitch.

Financing is available for implant treatment plans, and you'll see clear, complete pricing before anything begins.
