Common questions

Do you offer a membership plan for patients without insurance?+

Yes. Our in-house membership plan is $499 per year and includes two cleanings and two exams, plus 20% off all other services — no insurance paperwork and no surprises. Ask the team to enroll you at your visit.

Do you accept PPO insurance, and can you verify my benefits before my first visit?+

We file most PPO dental insurance and handle the claim for you. We'll check your specific plan and give you your estimated share before treatment, never after.

How often should I have a dental cleaning?+

Twice a year suits most people. Some patients with gum disease or a higher risk of decay benefit from more frequent visits, and we'll recommend an interval based on what we actually see rather than a default.
