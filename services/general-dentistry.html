General & family dentistry

Good dentistry starts with the unglamorous work done well: thorough exams, gentle cleanings, and honest conversations about what your teeth actually need — and what they don't.

What routine care looks like here

A typical visit includes a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays when they're due, an oral cancer screening, and a professional cleaning. Dr. Khan walks you through anything she finds in plain language, with photos where they help, so you understand your own mouth before any decision is made.

When treatment is needed — a filling, a crown, care for your gums — you'll get a clear plan and clear numbers first. We're committed to never recommending treatment you don't need; keeping small problems small is the whole point of coming in twice a year.

Families welcome

We see patients of every age, and we're patient with the nervous ones — kids and adults alike. Our team has been recognized by parents for taking extra time and care with children who find dental visits overwhelming, and we treat that as part of the job, not an interruption to it.

No insurance? Our in-house membership plan covers routine cleanings, exams, and X-rays for a flat fee — ask the team for current pricing.
