Sedation dentistry

Dental anxiety is real, common, and nothing to apologize for. Sedation options exist so that fear never has to be the reason a tooth goes untreated.

Comfort options, matched to you

Depending on your needs and health history, options may include nitrous oxide ("laughing gas") — which takes effect quickly, keeps you fully awake, and wears off within minutes — or oral sedation for deeper relaxation during longer visits. Which option fits is decided together at your exam, after reviewing your health history and the treatment planned.

Safety first, always

Sedation is administered conservatively and monitored throughout your visit. You'll receive clear instructions beforehand — including whether you'll need someone to drive you home — and the team checks on your comfort at every step.

Who it helps

Patients with dental anxiety, a strong gag reflex, difficulty getting numb, or several procedures being completed in one visit often benefit most. If you've been putting off care because of past experiences, say so — the plan will be built around your comfort, not despite it.

Nervous about a visit? Mention it when you book — comfort planning starts before you arrive. Same-day appointments are held during office hours for genuine urgencies.
