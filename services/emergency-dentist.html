Our services

Same-day & urgent dental care

Toothaches don't check your calendar. We hold time in our schedule for genuine urgencies — and we'll give you a straight answer about what needs to be seen today versus what can safely wait.

Call first — it's faster

For anything urgent, skip the form and call 817-926-1300. The team can triage on the phone, tell you what to do right now, and get you into a same-day slot when it's warranted. This is the visit patients remember — pain and swelling seen the same day, handled professionally and explained clearly.

What counts as urgent

Severe or worsening tooth pain, facial swelling, a knocked-out or broken tooth, bleeding that won't stop, or an abscess. If swelling ever affects your breathing or swallowing, that's beyond a dental office — go to the emergency room or call 911 first.

What to do while you head in

For a knocked-out adult tooth: handle it by the crown (not the root), keep it moist in milk or saliva, and get here fast — time matters. For pain, over-the-counter pain relief and a cold compress help; never place aspirin against the gum. For a broken tooth, save any pieces and rinse gently with warm water.

Common questions

Can I get a same-day dental appointment if I'm in pain or have swelling?+

Call 817-926-1300 first — we hold same-day appointments during office hours for genuine urgencies, and the team can triage on the phone and tell you what to do right now. If swelling affects your breathing or swallowing, go to the emergency room or call 911 instead.

What counts as a dental emergency?+

Severe or worsening tooth pain, facial swelling, a knocked-out or broken tooth, bleeding that won't stop, or an abscess. When you're unsure, call and describe what's happening — we'd rather tell you it can wait than have you sit with something that can't.

What should I do with a knocked-out tooth?+

Handle it by the crown, not the root, keep it moist in milk or saliva, and get to us quickly — time matters a great deal for whether the tooth can be saved.
