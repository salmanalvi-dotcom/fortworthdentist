Teeth whitening

Coffee, tea, wine, and years do their work on every smile. Professional whitening undoes it — safely, evenly, and dramatically faster than anything from a drugstore shelf.

Why professional whitening wins

Dentist-supervised whitening uses stronger, safer formulations with custom-fit trays that keep the gel on your teeth and off your gums. The result: shades of improvement in days, not months, without the sensitivity roulette of one-size-fits-all strips.

Honest expectations

Whitening lightens natural enamel — it doesn't change the color of crowns, veneers, or fillings, and some stains (like tetracycline) respond less. Dr. Khan will look at your teeth and tell you honestly what whitening can achieve for you, and whether another approach would serve you better.

Thinking bigger than color?

If the shape or proportion of your teeth bothers you as much as the shade, that's a smile-design conversation — Dr. Khan's boutique studio, The Veneer Suite, focuses on minimal prep porcelain veneers.

Common questions

How is dentist-supervised whitening different from strips?+

Stronger formulations, custom-fit trays that hold the gel evenly against your teeth, and a check of your teeth and gums first. Results show in days rather than months, without the sensitivity roulette of one-size-fits-all strips.

Will whitening work on my crowns, veneers or fillings?+

No. Whitening lightens natural enamel only. If you have visible dental work at the front, we plan around it so everything matches.

Are there stains whitening won't fix?+

Some, such as tetracycline staining, respond less. The consultation tells you what result to expect before you commit.

Will my teeth be sensitive?+

Some people notice temporary sensitivity for a day or two. Custom trays and a controlled concentration reduce it, and the schedule can be adjusted.

How long do results last?+

It depends on coffee, tea, wine and tobacco. Regular cleanings and an occasional touch-up with your trays keep teeth bright longer.
