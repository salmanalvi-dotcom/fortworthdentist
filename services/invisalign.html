Invisalign

Straighter teeth without brackets and wires: Invisalign moves your teeth with a series of clear, removable aligners — nearly invisible, and out of your way when you eat.

Why patients choose it

The aligners are discreet enough for work and photos, removable for meals and brushing, and typically more comfortable than traditional braces. Treatment is planned digitally from a 3D scan of your teeth, which means you preview the projected result before you commit to anything.

A Gold Provider's difference

Dr. Khan is an Invisalign Gold Provider — a designation based on case experience. That experience matters in the planning stage: knowing which movements aligners do beautifully, which cases need refinements, and how to sequence treatment so the result holds.

What treatment looks like

After your scan and plan, you'll wear each aligner set about two weeks, with brief check-ins to keep things on track. Many cases finish in 6 to 18 months depending on complexity. Afterward, retainers protect the investment — we'll set you up properly rather than letting the result drift.
