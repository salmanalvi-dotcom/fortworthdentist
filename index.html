Dental implants

Missing teeth, permanently solved.

An implant replaces the whole tooth — root and crown — so it looks, feels, and works like your own. Dr. Khan is Implant Continuum trained with over 2,000 hours of continuing education across implantology and restorative dentistry, and every case is planned digitally before anything is placed.

Whether it's one tooth, several, or a full arch, you'll get a clear plan, clear pricing, and a straight answer about whether an implant is truly your best option.

Common questions

Are you accepting new patients?+

Yes — for general, family, implant, and Invisalign care. Request an appointment below or call and the team will find you a time.

Where are you located?+

We're currently at 1612 Pennsylvania Ave, Fort Worth. In late summer 2026 we move to our new purpose-built studio at 416 S Henderson St — same doctors, same phone number, new home.

Do you take my insurance?+

We accept most PPO dental plans and verify your benefits before treatment. Call with your card handy and we'll check for you before your first visit.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?+

Most adults with a missing tooth or failing tooth are — candidacy depends on gum health and bone, which we assess with digital imaging at your consultation. If an implant isn't your best option, Dr. Khan will say so and explain the alternatives.

I have a toothache — can you see me quickly?+

Call us first at 817-926-1300 — we hold time for genuine urgencies and will give you honest guidance on what needs to be seen today versus what can safely wait.

Do you do veneers and cosmetic work?+

Yes — cosmetic smile design lives at our boutique studio, The Veneer Suite, where Dr. Khan focuses on no-shave and minimal-prep porcelain veneers. Visit fortworthveneersuite.com to see her cases.
